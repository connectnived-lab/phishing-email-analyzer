Why this score

    How the score works

    Sender

    Reply-To and Return-Path pointing elsewhere, brand names in the sender name, look-alike sender domains, failed SPF / DKIM / DMARC.

    Links

    Link text that shows one address but opens another, IP-address links, URL shorteners, punycode and look-alike brand domains, risky domain endings, @ tricks.

    Wording

    Urgency and threats, requests for passwords / OTP / card details, KYC and blocked-account scams, lottery and prize lures.

    Attachments

    Programs and scripts (.exe, .js, .iso …), disguised double extensions, macro-enabled Office files, HTML login pages, archives.

    Each warning adds points. The total (capped at 100) gives the verdict. This is a teaching and triage aid: a low score does not prove an email is safe, so when in doubt, ask your IT team.
